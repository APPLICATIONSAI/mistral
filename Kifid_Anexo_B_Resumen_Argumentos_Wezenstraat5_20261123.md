# Annex — Summary of Grounds (Arguments in Favour)

**Geschillencommissie Financiële Dienstverlening (Kifid) — summary of the Complainant's grounds**

**Complainant:** Mrs Patricia Quinche Guerra · **Claim:** CVBS2632111 (water damage, 26 June 2026) · **Policies:** 5410V043736 (Gebouw uitgebreid + Glas) and 5410V043737 (Bedrijfsaansprakelijkheid)
**Respondents:** Söderberg & Partners Volmacht B.V., Van Loon Verzekeringen, Avéro Achmea / Achmea Schadeverzekeringen N.V.

## 1. The Respondents confirmed in writing — twice — that this damage was covered (strongest ground)

After the loss, the Complainant asked Van Loon in writing what "leegstand" (vacancy) means for her policy and invited Van Loon to correct her written record of its answer. Instead of correcting it, Van Loon **confirmed it in writing twice**: on **7 July 2026** ("I can confirm you that your response to A and B is wright") and again on **9 July 2026** ("YES") — namely that after three months of vacancy *other damages are still covered* besides fire and storm, and that clause V5069 (owner's interest: floors, kitchens, sanitary fittings, up to EUR 5,000) applies to her floor and kitchen damage. The loss is exactly that: water damage to floor, walls and kitchen.

She was entitled to rely on these written answers from the party administering her policy (Art. 3:35 and 3:61 BW). Van Loon's own e-mail of 8 October 2026 concedes in writing that the information given "does not appear to be fully consistent" with its later position and "may be perceived as contradictory". Söderberg's explanation (a "commercial colleague not involved in claims handling") does not cure it: the 2 July rejection, on Söderberg's own account, was never communicated to her, and its own agent kept confirming coverage on 7 and 9 July.

## 2. The vacancy limitation, as worded, does not clearly apply — and any doubt favours the insured

"Leegstand" is **defined nowhere** in the policy documents: not in the policy schedule, not in Art. 8 (which treats vacancy only as a reportable change of risk), not in Art. 9 (valuation) nor in Art. 11.2.2.3. The policy schedule itself is truncated in her copy ("…uitslu"). Under the **Haviltex** standard (Art. 6:248(1) BW) for the policy schedule, and Art. 6:238(2) BW for the VBB 2025-01 conditions, ambiguity is construed in the consumer's favour. The Respondents' own staff misread the clause in July 2026 — in the exact opposite sense to their current position — which demonstrates that a lay client could not be expected to understand it.

Further article-by-article analysis (Annex C — Policy analysis, attached) establishes, on the Respondents' own policy documents:

- **The insurer knew of the vacancy from the start** (Art. 8.2: the insurer is aware of the use of the property at the commencement) and the policy schedule itself records the destination as "Leegstaand Winkelpand": vacancy was the accepted basis of the contract, not an unreported change of risk (Art. 8.3 cannot be invoked against her);
- **Art. 3.8 (water) contains no vacancy exclusion**; the only peril the conditions restrict for buildings out of use is vandalism (Art. 3.14) — where the insurer wanted to restrict a peril, it said so expressly (argument a contrario);
- **Arts. 9 and 11.2.2.3 are valuation rules**, not peril limitations — so Van Loon's answer of 2 July 2026, referring to Arts. 8/9 as the meaning of "leegstand", was factually wrong;
- **Van Loon's letter of 11 September 2026** ("the conditions clearly state that… water damage is not insured", "duidelijk vermeld") **is contradicted by the text of the conditions themselves**;
- **The policy schedule is internally inconsistent** ("Type bedrijfspand: Woonpand" vs "Bestemming: Leegstaand Winkelpand"), consistent with a standard product configured without care (see 3).

## 3. Duty of care: a product that could never respond to the risk she declared

The intermediary knew from its own e-mail of **9 May 2022** that she bought the property "for renting out" and that it was empty. It nevertheless:

- sold her — as a **single closed proposal, without advice** — a "Gebouw uitgebreid" policy whose extended cover (water, theft, vandalism) was switched off by the very state she had declared, and never explained that practical consequence to a lay, foreign client (Art. 4:20, 4:23 Wft; Art. 7:401 BW; Art. 6:193b–193d BW: misleading omission); comparable case: Kifid 2024-0504, 18 June 2024;
- renewed it for four years without ever reviewing whether it still fitted a property it knew remained empty (the duty of care is continuous);
- charged premiums throughout for cover that could not respond while the unit was vacant (e.g. glass, EUR 50.24/year);
- never proposed any alternative — although dedicated vacancy products and wordings without an automatic vacancy exclusion existed in the market (De Vereende DV BLB, OOM Combinatiepolis water module, Zicht VPE 2012, De Goudse 770655; enquiries of 19 November 2026, annexes) — and never recommended the standard preventive measure that would have prevented this exact loss at no cost: **closing the main water supply**;
- has produced **no client profile, needs analysis or advice report** (request 6), despite asserting that she "understood" the consequences; the contemporaneous record shows the opposite — she asked for explanations (4 August 2022: "Why I should inform about it?… Thanks in advance for your explanation") and received only the rule repeated.

## 4. Causation: proper advice would have changed the outcome

She states expressly that, if properly informed, she would not have accepted the offer as it stood and would have obtained cover including water damage or protected the unit herself. Her conduct supports this: she insured immediately after purchase, asked about the practical content and cost of each cover before deciding (10 May, 3 August 2022), and is doing exactly that now (written market enquiries of 19 November 2026). Unlike Kifid 2024-0504, she does not rely on market prices alone; the measure that would most likely have avoided this loss (closing the stopcock) cost nothing. If certainty is not accepted, she claims the lost chance (verlies van een kans).

## 5. Mitigation costs are recoverable independently (Art. 7:957 BW; Art. 5.1 VBB)

The emergency repair, water extraction, drying and partial kitchen dismantling were legally required to limit damage (Art. 7:957(1) BW) and reported as they happened. Their reasonable cost (EUR 5,445) is recoverable under Art. 7:957(2) BW where the insured could reasonably fear the loss was covered — and her grounds could not have been stronger: the Respondents' own written confirmations of 7 and 9 July 2026. The same measures moreover protected perils admittedly covered *(fire and storm)* and the neighbouring unit, whose damage is fully resolved: the neighbour's own insurer paid it, and the difference between the amounts covered under the two policies was paid without objection under the liability policy 5410V043737. No ruling is sought on the neighbour's claim.

## 6. Premium refund, whatever the outcome on causation

For four years she paid for extended perils and glass — flagged in the 2026 schedule as inapplicable during vacancy — knowledge of which the Respondents had from the start. She claims, in any event, the yearly difference between the premium paid and that of fire-and-storm cover for a vacant shop unit (request 5). She accepts this refund may be offset against any other award.

## 7. Claims handling failures after the loss

Three months with no guidance despite two written requests through the Respondents' own portal (14 July: acknowledged, "z.s.m.", never answered); rejection on 2 September **without any inspection**, followed by an assertion that no progress is possible without one; circular referrals (Van Loon → Söderberg → Van Loon) with the question "is vacancy the sole ground?" repeatedly asked in writing and never directly answered; and a "gesture of goodwill" visit whose purpose (investigating the technical cause) she learned from the loss adjuster, not from Van Loon — illogical, because vacancy excludes water damage whatever its cause (Art. 6:2, 6:248 BW; Gedragscode Behandeling Claims). The difficulty of investigating the site now is the consequence of the Respondents' own delay and cannot be held against an insured who was legally obliged to mitigate.

## 8. Relief requested

- **Main:** the claim is covered and payable under the policy — **EUR 21,316** (kitchen capped at the EUR 5,000 of V5069; floor and wall repair falls under Art. 3.8, outside that cap), less own risk EUR 250, with statutory interest.
- **Subsidiary:** breach of the duty of care in the advice, sale, renewal and information — **EUR 25,816** (kitchen at full cost), or the lost chance, if causation is not held certain.
- **In any event:** refund of the premiums for cover that could not respond during vacancy (request 5), with statutory interest.
- Plus: production of the 2022 advice records and a legible policy schedule (request 6), and identification of the insurer and decision-maker.
